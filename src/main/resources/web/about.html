About sift

sift is an independent web search engine, built from scratch as an open-source project.

Privacy

  • No accounts, no cookies, no ads and no tracking.
  • Searches are not logged or stored, and are not linked to you.
  • When you open a result, the site you visit is not told what you searched for.
  • Your theme choice is the only thing kept, in your own browser.

Where results come from

Results come from public web pages: sites visited by our crawler, pages from the Common Crawl open archive, and Wikipedia. Pages are ranked by how well their text, title and address match your search, how other pages link to them, and how widely used their site is. Nobody can pay to be ranked higher.

For site owners

Our crawler identifies itself as siftbot/1.0 (+https://github.com/iPriyadarshi/SearchEngine). It follows robots.txt, makes at most one request per second to a site (slower if you set Crawl-delay), and reads only a limited number of pages per site. To keep it out of your site: 

User-agent: siftbot
Disallow: /

Pages marked noindex are never indexed. To have a page removed or to report a problem, open an issue with the page address.

Search tips

  • Put words in quotes to find an exact phrase: "black hole"
  • Combine words with capitalised AND, OR, NOT: jazz AND NOT rock
  • Press / to jump to the search box.
